Built to be trusted

Every transfer is watched, every login is checked.

OTP + biometric login

A fresh one-time code is pushed to your registered number or email for new devices and sensitive actions. Return visits can use your phone's fingerprint or Face ID instead.

Card linking, encrypted

Card details are tokenized the moment they're added — Xborder Pay never stores raw card numbers, and any linked card can be frozen or removed instantly.

Transaction monitoring

Unusual amounts, new recipients, or new countries trigger extra verification and, where needed, a manual review before funds move.

Full rate transparency

Every conversion shows the exact rate and fee, locked for 60 seconds, before you confirm — nothing recalculated after the fact.

Compliance & licensing

The unglamorous part that makes it safe to trust us.

A payments network only works if regulators, banks, and card networks trust it too.

📜

Licensed money transmitter

Operating under money transmission / payment institution licenses in each market served, in partnership with local regulators.

💳

PCI DSS Level 1

Card data is handled by a PCI DSS Level 1 certified processing partner — raw card numbers never touch our own servers.

🛡️

AML / CFT program

Sanctions-list screening, transaction monitoring, and suspicious-activity reporting run on every account and every transfer.

🔐

Data protection

Encryption in transit and at rest, aligned to ISO 27001 practices, with data handled per applicable regional privacy law.

Note on this prototype: the badges above represent the licensing and certifications a live cross-border payments product would need to obtain — they are shown here as placeholders to illustrate the requirement, not as claims about a real, currently licensed entity.
